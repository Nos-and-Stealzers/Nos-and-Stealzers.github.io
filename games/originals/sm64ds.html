🎮 Super Mario 64 DS

This runs a Nintendo DS emulator (EmulatorJS) right in your browser. It needs a Super Mario 64 DS ROM file (.nds). For copyright reasons the ROM isn't bundled — point it at a ROM URL, or load one from your device. Save states are kept on this device automatically.

Tip: put a ROM at games/roms/sm64ds.nds in the repo and it loads automatically.
