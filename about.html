What is this place?

Arcade Campus Hub is a collection of games you can play straight in your browser. No downloads, no installs, no launcher — you click a game and it plays. Everything runs on ordinary websites, so it works on laptops, Chromebooks and most school or work networks where game sites usually don't.

It's made to be simple: browse the index, pick something that looks fun, play. Your favourites, recent games and playtime are remembered on your device automatically.

What's on offer

  • A big games index — arcade, action, racing, puzzle, sports and more, sorted into categories with a search box and a random-pick button.
  • Minecraft in your browser — three versions, no account or download needed: a 1.12.2 build (the recommended one), a light 1.8.8 build that runs on anything, a newer 26.1.2 build with modern blocks (bigger first download, cached after that), and Minecraft Classic.
  • PatentSMP — our own Minecraft server. In the browser versions it's already in the multiplayer list, so joining is one click. Playing from real Minecraft (Java Edition, 1.8.8 to latest) works too — the address is on the Minecraft page.
  • Optional accounts — sign up to keep your progress in the cloud, add friends, chat, and voice or video call while you play.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants a quick game without installing anything — students on a locked-down laptop, people on a break at work, or anyone who just likes browser games. Some games run better than others depending on your network; the index marks each title with a small reliability flag so you know what to expect before you click.

Getting started

  • 1. Open the index and pick a game — that's really it. Most titles launch instantly.
  • 2. Want your progress to follow you? Create a free account. Signing up adopts everything you've already played on that device, so nothing resets.
  • 3. Playing Minecraft? Head to the Minecraft page and pick a version — PatentSMP is already wired in.

Accounts and saves

You never need an account to play. But if you move between devices — or a friend borrows a different computer — an account keeps your game progress safe in the cloud and brings it back wherever you sign in. Two devices always merge instead of overwriting, so signing in somewhere new can't wipe your progress.

Your email is only used to confirm your account and recover your password — it's never shown to other players. You can export your data as a file, or delete your account entirely, any time from Settings.

FAQ

  • Is it free? — Yes. Every game on the index is free to play, and accounts cost nothing too.
  • Do I need to install anything? — No. Everything runs in the browser, including Minecraft.
  • Will my progress survive? — On your device, always. In the cloud, if you make an account. Progress from before you signed up is adopted, not lost.
  • Is Minecraft really playable here? — Yes, three versions run in the browser, and they can all join our PatentSMP server with one click.
  • Does it work on a Chromebook? — Yes, that's half the point. On weaker machines pick the light 1.8.8 Minecraft build.
  • Something's broken or unfair — who do I tell? — Every profile and conversation has a report button, and there's a feedback form in the sidebar. Reports are read by a person, not a bot.
  • Do you track me? — No analytics, no ad trackers, no third-party cookies. Your settings live in this browser; your game saves live with your account.

The short version

Free browser games, Minecraft included, with a server of our own on the side. Make an account if you want your progress everywhere — or don't, and just play. Games are third-party works owned by their creators and provided as-is.
